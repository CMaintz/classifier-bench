# Imported datasets

Sampled cases in this directory come from these sources, under their own licenses.

- `banking77`: legacy-datasets/banking77 (test, revision f54121560de4), CC-BY-4.0. Casanueva et al. 2020, PolyAI (BANKING77).
- `civil_comments`: google/civil_comments (test, revision f2970eb3a557), CC0-1.0. Borkan et al. 2019, Jigsaw / Civil Comments.
- `clinc_bank_scope`: clinc/clinc_oos (test, revision 155b9c710419), CC-BY-3.0. Larson et al. 2019, CLINC150 (clinc/oos-eval).
- `dkhate`: mteb/DKHateClassification (test, revision de60cf0845d8), CC-BY-4.0. Sigurbergsson and Derczynski 2020 (DKHate).
- `goemotions_ekman`: google-research-datasets/go_emotions (test, revision add492243ff9), Apache-2.0. Demszky et al. 2020, Google (GoEmotions).
- `jailbreak`: jackhhao/jailbreak-classification (test, revision 2f2ceeb39658), Apache-2.0. Jack Hhao (jackhhao/jailbreak-classification).
- `language_id_public`: mteb/amazon_massive_intent (test, revision 940fd47a81ea), CC-BY-4.0. FitzGerald et al. 2022, Amazon (MASSIVE).
- `massive_scenario_da`: mteb/amazon_massive_scenario (test, revision 58871793b91a), CC-BY-4.0. FitzGerald et al. 2022, Amazon (MASSIVE).
- `paws_paraphrase`: google-research-datasets/paws (test, revision 161ece9501cf), Free for any purpose (Google, PAWS). Zhang, Baldridge and He 2019, Google (PAWS).
- `sms_spam`: ucirvine/sms_spam (train, revision cae486f927c2), CC-BY-4.0. Almeida and Gomez Hidalgo 2011, UCI SMS Spam Collection.
